Øqesträ

A music player for Android, made for people who own their music.

Your files, organised beautifully. A ten-band equaliser, a visualiser that takes its colours from the album art, and a queue you can actually see. No ads, no account, nothing leaves your phone.

  1. 1

    Your whole library

    Albums, songs and artists from the music on your phone, with search across everything.

  2. 2

    Folder playlists

    Turn any folder into a playlist. Add songs to the folder and the playlist follows.

  3. 3

    Ten-band equaliser

    31 Hz to 16 kHz, nine presets, a live response curve and a preamp that keeps boosts clean.

  4. 4

    A visualiser that matches the album

    Bars, Wave or Orbit, coloured from the cover art so every record gets its own mood.

  5. 5

    A queue you can see

    Slide up to see what plays next, in the real order, even on shuffle.

  6. 6

    Plays in the background

    Notification and lock-screen controls, headset buttons, and it pauses when you unplug.
