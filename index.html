Øqesträ

A music player for Android, made for people who own their music.

Your files, organised beautifully. Its own sound engine, an equaliser with headphone correction for about 6,000 models, and a visualiser that takes its colours from the album art. No ads, no account, nothing leaves your phone.

Download for Android See the screens

Test build 0.6.2 for Android 8.0 or newer, about 27 MB. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser. The Google Play release follows in 2027.

In the app today

Everything in one quiet place.

  • Albums
  • Songs
  • Artists
  • Playlists
  1. 1

    Your whole library

    Albums, songs and artists from the music on your phone, search across everything, any folder as a playlist, and a day or dark look: white on black at night.

  2. 2

    Its own sound engine

    Oqestra decodes your files itself and processes in 64-bit, with gapless playback. Lossless music leaves the engine bit for bit, and when your phone or headphones run at another sample rate, Oqestra converts at mastering quality instead of Android.

  3. 3

    An equaliser for every pair

    Graphic or parametric, with headphone correction for about 6,000 models. Each speaker, headphone and DAC keeps its own settings.

  4. 4

    A visualiser that matches the album

    Five patterns and eight themes, coloured from the cover art so every record gets its own mood. Tap it to play, pause or skip.

  5. 5

    Widgets in three styles

    White, frosted glass or monochrome: album art edge to edge, in Oqestra’s own type, with play, pause and skip. Sizes 2×2, 4×1, 4×2 and 4×4.

  6. 6

    Bit-perfect USB

    On Android 14 and newer, your USB DAC gets the file untouched. Open Sound to see every step from file to DAC.
