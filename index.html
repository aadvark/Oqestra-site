Why Oqestra

The audiophile engine and the player you want to look at, in one app.

Most listeners run two players: one that sounds right and one that looks right. Oqestra is built to be both.

ENGINE

Bit-perfect by default

Our own USB driver sends every bit of the file to your DAC untouched, up to 32-bit/768 kHz and native DSD. On Android 14 and later it uses the system's bit-perfect path too.

LIBRARY

One library, every source

Files on your phone, your NAS over SMB or WebDAV, and Jellyfin, Plex or Navidrome servers, merged into one library with duplicates folded together.

SIGNAL

See what reaches the DAC

The signal-path inspector shows every step from file to output, and tells you plainly when playback is bit-perfect and when something is changing the sound.
